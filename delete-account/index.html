Delete your account

Spottr, by Medas Girdzius

In the app

Open Spottr, go to Account, then Delete account, and confirm. Your account and its backup are removed from our servers straight away. If you signed in with Apple, you confirm with Apple first.

Without the app

Email support@spottr.me from the address you sign in with, with the subject “Delete my Spottr account”. We delete the account and its backup within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: email address and sign-in details
  • Your cloud backup: workouts, routines, body weights, goals and settings

What stays

  • Data on your phone. Spottr works offline, so your workouts stay on the phone until you choose Account → Delete all data or delete the app.
  • Purchase records. Apple or Google keep the receipt for any purchase, and our purchase processor keeps a record of it under a random ID that is no longer linked to your email, for tax and accounting.
  • Anonymous usage statistics, which were never linked to you or your account.

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

Questions
