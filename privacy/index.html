Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

The short version

Spottr works without an account, and then your training data never leaves your phone. If you choose to sign in, we keep a backup of your training data and your email address so you can get it back on another phone. To see what to improve, Spottr sends anonymous usage statistics, which you can turn off. There are no ads and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

Your workouts, routines, exercises, body weight entries, profile details (sex, age and height, if you add them) and settings are saved in a database on your device only. We never receive them.

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email. We then store:

  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple, this can be a private relay address Apple creates for you), so you can sign in again;
  • a backup copy of your workouts (every set, including warm-ups, and your exercise notes), routines, custom exercises, body weight entries, lift goals, plan builder answers, profile details and settings, so you can restore them on another phone. A workout still in progress is only backed up once you finish it.

The backup is updated a few seconds after you change something. It is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers protected so that only your account can read it. We use it only to give you your data back.

How your details are used

Sex, age, height and body weight are optional. Spottr uses them on your phone to rank your lifts, work out your strength score and estimate calories and protein. These estimates are general guidance, not medical advice.

Purchases

Spottr Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. It receives an anonymous identifier (or your account ID if you're signed in) and your purchase history for Spottr, nothing else.

Usage statistics

Spottr sends anonymous usage statistics to PostHog, our analytics provider: which screens are opened, when the app is opened, and events such as a workout started or finished (with its length and number of exercises and sets), a routine created or the Pro screen viewed. They are tied to a random identifier created on your phone, never to your account, email or name, and never include what you lift, your notes or your body details. If the app hits an error, it also sends a crash report: the error message, where in Spottr's code it happened and which screen was open, tied to the same random identifier. We don't collect your location. We use all of this only to see which features help, what to improve and what to fix.

To turn them off, crash reports included, open Account and switch off Share usage data.

App updates

Spottr checks for and downloads small fixes from Expo, the service that delivers them. That request contains the app version and type of phone, not your data.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Spottr schedules local reminders on your phone: when your rest timer ends, a workout reminder after 2 days without training (you can turn it off in Account) and, on the free plan, before your oldest workouts reach the 6-month history limit. These are created on the device; no notification data leaves your phone.

Exports

If you choose to export your data, Spottr creates a file and opens the share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely up to you. Exports include your routines, custom exercises, weigh-ins, goals and settings. On the free plan, they include workouts and weigh-ins from your last 3 months.

How long workouts are kept

Spottr never deletes your workouts. On the free plan, workouts and weigh-ins older than 3 months are locked: they stay stored on your phone (and in your backup if you're signed in), but you can't open or export them until you get Spottr Pro. Before your oldest workouts are locked, the app tells you a month, two weeks, a week and a day ahead. With Pro, everything is open for as long as you use the app.

Deleting your data

In the app, Account → Delete account removes your account and its backup from our servers straight away. Account → Delete all data erases everything on the phone, as does deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play and must be cancelled there. Without the app, see how to delete your account.

Testing

While Spottr is in beta, Apple's TestFlight and Google Play's testing programmes may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. That is handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Spottr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: email support@spottr.me.
