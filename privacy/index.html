Spottr

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

The short version

Spottr works without an account, and then nothing leaves your phone. If you choose to sign in, we keep a backup of your training data and your email address so you can get it back on another phone. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell your data.

Without an account

Your workouts, routines, exercises, body weight entries and settings are saved in a database on your device only. We never receive them.

If you sign in (optional)

You can sign in with Apple or with a code sent to your email. We then store:

  • your email address (with Sign in with Apple, this can be a private relay address Apple creates for you), so you can sign in again;
  • a backup copy of your workouts, routines, custom exercises, body weight entries and settings, so you can restore them on another phone.

The backup is updated a few seconds after you change something. It is stored with our hosting provider, Supabase, on servers protected so that only your account can read it. We use it only to give you your data back.

Purchases

Spottr Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active. It receives an anonymous identifier (or your account ID if you're signed in) and your purchase history for Spottr, nothing else.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Spottr schedules a local reminder on your phone when your rest timer ends. These are created on the device; no notification data leaves your phone.

Exports

If you choose to export your data, Spottr creates a file and opens the share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely up to you.

Deleting your data

In the app, Account → Delete account removes your account and its backup from our servers straight away. Account → Delete all data erases everything on the phone, as does deleting the app. Subscriptions are managed by the App Store or Google Play and must be cancelled there.

Testing

While Spottr is in beta, Apple's TestFlight and Google Play's testing programmes may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. That is handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies.

Children

Spottr is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions or deletion requests: use the feedback option in TestFlight or on the App Store page.
