Spottr

Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

The short version

Spottr does not collect any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

Your workouts, routines, exercises, body weight entries and settings are saved in a database on your device only. We never receive them, and they are not sent to any server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Spottr schedules a local reminder on your phone when your rest timer ends. These are created on the device; no notification data leaves your phone.

Exports

If you choose to export your data, Spottr creates a file and opens the iOS share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely up to you.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything from Settings in the app, or by deleting the app from your iPhone.

TestFlight

While Spottr is in beta, Apple's TestFlight may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer. That is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Spottr is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions? Use the feedback option in TestFlight or the App Store page.
